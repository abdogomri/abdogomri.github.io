FieldStamp — Terms of Sale and Use

Last updated: 29 July 2026

What is free

Taking photographs, stamping them with the time, coordinates and address, organising them into sites, exporting a ZIP of the images and exporting a CSV of the metadata are free and remain free. They carry no advertising, no watermark and no photo count limit.

What FieldStamp Pro adds

Pro unlocks the PDF report: cover page, summary table and paginated photographs — the document you hand to a client.

The two ways to buy

Pro is offered as a one-time lifetime purchase or as an annual subscription. The lifetime purchase is not a subscription: it is billed once and never renews. Prices are shown inside the app in your own currency, taxes included where applicable.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

All payments go through Google Play. The annual subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You cancel it in the Google Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions — not inside FieldStamp, because we have no ability to charge or refund you ourselves.

Refunds

Refunds are governed by Google Play's refund policy and are requested from Google. If a purchase fails, no amount is charged.

Restoring a purchase

Your entitlement is attached to your Google account, not to your handset. Reinstalling the app or changing phone restores it: open the Pro screen and tap Restore.

What the app does and does not guarantee

FieldStamp records the position reported by your device, together with its accuracy margin, and refuses to shoot while that position is not usable. It cannot guarantee that a satellite fix is correct, and it is not a surveying instrument or a legally certified measuring device. A photograph imported from your gallery is marked as imported and is never presented as proving a place.

You are responsible for keeping your own backups of the files the app produces. They live on your device only.

Acceptable use

Do not use FieldStamp to photograph people or property where you have no right to do so, and respect the law that applies where you are working.
