Terrain Tactical Watch Face

Privacy policy

7 November 2026 · Magic apps & games

Terrain Tactical Watch Face displays the time, date, time format and battery level provided by your Wear OS watch. Your watch's weather service may provide local weather conditions and temperature. A Wear OS complication provider may provide step counts and progress toward a step goal. The weather service may use your device's location to provide local conditions. Availability and permissions depend on your watch and the relevant providers.

The watch face does not itself request location or network access or send the displayed information to the publisher. The publisher does not collect, receive, store or share these values. The watch face has no account, advertising or analytics SDK.

Wear OS, the weather service and other complication providers handle their data under their own settings and privacy terms. Their services may use location or other device data independently of the watch face.

Google Play handles purchases and installation under its own privacy policy. You can remove the watch face through Wear OS. To ask about privacy or deletion, contact abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.
