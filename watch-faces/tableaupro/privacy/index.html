Privacy policy

7 November 2026 · Magic apps & games

Tableau Pro uses the time, date, time format and battery level provided by your Wear OS watch to display the watch face. Wear OS complication providers may also supply step counts and, when available and permitted, calendar event information for display on your watch. Availability and permissions depend on your device and the provider.

The publisher does not collect, receive, store or share this information. The resource-only watch face has no account, network permission, advertising or analytics SDK. The providers and operating system handle their data under their own policies and settings.

Google Play handles purchases and installation under its own privacy policy. You can remove the watch face through Wear OS. To ask about privacy or deletion, contact abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.
