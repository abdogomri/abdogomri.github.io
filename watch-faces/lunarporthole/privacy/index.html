Lunar Porthole

Privacy policy

7 November 2026 · Magic apps & games

Lunar Porthole uses the time, date, time format, moon phase and battery level provided by your Wear OS watch to display the watch face. It can also display a step count supplied by a system complication provider. These values are displayed on the watch.

The watch face has no account, network permission, advertising or analytics SDK. The publisher does not collect, receive, store or share the information displayed by the watch face.

Step information is supplied by the watch platform or its data provider, which may use its own permissions and privacy settings. Availability depends on your watch and its software. Consult the applicable provider's privacy policy for its handling of this information.

Google Play handles purchases and installation under its own privacy policy. You can remove the watch face through Wear OS. To ask about privacy or deletion, contact abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.
