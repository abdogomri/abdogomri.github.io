Aurore Solar

Privacy policy

7 November 2026 · Magic apps & games

Aurore Solar displays the time, date, time format and battery level provided by your Wear OS watch. It can also display steps and the next sunrise or sunset supplied by system complication providers. These values are displayed on the watch.

The watch face itself does not request your location or send the displayed information to the publisher. The publisher does not collect, receive, store or share these values. The watch face has no account, network permission, advertising or analytics SDK.

Wear OS and its complication providers may use device information, including location, to supply their data; their own privacy terms and settings apply. The availability of sunrise or sunset information depends on your device and its provider.

Google Play handles purchases and installation under its own privacy policy. You can remove the watch face through Wear OS. To ask about privacy or deletion, contact abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.
