Privacy policy · 28 October 2026

Your watch, your information.

This policy covers the watch faces published by Magic apps & games and, where provided, their Android phone installation companions.

Contact: abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.

Applications covered

Observatoire, Atelier, Signal, Tempo, Méridien, Émail and Botanique Nocturne, including their phone companions. This policy also covers Monolithe (com.observatoire.monolithe), Papier (com.observatoire.papier) and Nixie Lab (com.observatoire.nixielab), which are standalone watch faces without a phone companion, as well as Tilo (com.observatoire.tilo), a standalone step-linked watch face. Paco is also covered as a standalone watch face without a phone companion. This policy also covers Petit Express (com.observatoire.petitexpress), a standalone step-linked watch face.

Monolithe and Papier: local watch display

These two watch faces use Watch Face Format: Wear OS renders their resources without application code or an embedded SDK. They display the system time, date and watch battery level. Monolithe also has a customizable text complication that displays information supplied by the provider you select, such as your next calendar event. That provider manages its own permissions and privacy practices. Papier does not display calendar or health information.

Monolithe and Papier make no network requests, contain no advertising or analytics SDK, and do not collect or share user data. They require no publisher account. Display values and appearance settings are processed locally by Wear OS; Magic apps & games does not receive them or keep their history. The phone companion and Wear SDK diagnostics described below do not apply to these two watch faces. Google Play handles purchases and downloads under its own privacy policy.

Clair, Altitude, Intervalle, Rivage, Nacre and Floraison

These standalone watch faces (com.observatoire.clair, .altitude, .intervalle, .rivage, .nacre and .floraison) have no phone companion, application code, analytics, advertising SDK, network permission or publisher account. Wear OS renders their appearance and processes their settings locally. Depending on the design, they show time, date, watch battery, steps, a daily step goal chosen in the editor, weather, the next solar event or calendar information from the system or a configured complication provider. Magic apps & games does not receive, upload, store or share these displayed values. Calendar, weather and activity providers manage their own permissions and privacy practices. These watch faces do not diagnose or treat health conditions. The phone companion diagnostics described below do not apply to them.

Élan, Cadence and Prisme Hub

These standalone digital watch faces (com.observatoire.elan, com.observatoire.cadence and com.observatoire.prismehub) use Watch Face Format without application code, a phone companion, network permission, advertising or analytics SDK, or a publisher account. Wear OS processes time, date, battery, appearance and shortcut settings locally. Configurable complications display data supplied by the providers selected on the watch, such as step count, calendar text, battery or progress. A tap may open the provider’s own action or the watch’s alarms, settings, media controls or battery screen. Those applications manage their own permissions and data handling. Magic apps & games does not receive, upload, store or share these displayed values or a history of shortcut use. These watch faces do not diagnose or treat health conditions. Google Play handles purchases and downloads; the phone companion diagnostics below do not apply to these three watch faces.

Nixie Lab

Nixie Lab (com.observatoire.nixielab) is a standalone Wear OS watch face built with Watch Face Format. It contains no application code, phone companion, network permission, advertising or analytics SDK, or publisher account. Wear OS processes its displayed time, date, battery level and appearance settings locally. Magic apps & games does not receive, upload, store or share these values. Google Play handles purchases and downloads under its own privacy policy.

Paco: an animated companion, data on your watch

Paco is a standalone Wear OS watch face. Wear OS renders the time, date, daily step count and goal supplied by the watch’s activity provider. The step count and goal are used only to display daily progress and choose the same dog’s animated reactions. Paco does not create an activity history or save cumulative growth. Wear OS processes the displayed data and appearance settings locally; Magic apps & games does not receive, upload, retain or share your step count, goal or their history.

The face contains only Watch Face Format resources, without application code or an embedded SDK. It has no phone companion, publisher account, advertising or analytics, and requests no network or health permission. This policy’s descriptions of phone companions and their diagnostics do not apply to Paco. Wear OS and the activity provider control data availability and have their own privacy practices. You can manage those services in their settings and uninstall Paco; removing the face does not delete another provider’s activity history. Google Play handles purchases and downloads under its own policy. This page’s support-email terms apply to messages you choose to send to abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.

Petit Express: steps stay on your watch

Petit Express (com.observatoire.petitexpress) is a standalone Wear OS watch face built with Watch Face Format. Wear OS displays the system time and date and the daily step count supplied by the watch’s activity provider. The steps are used only to render today’s count and progress toward the displayed 8,000-step goal. The decorative train animation is independent of step activity. The count resets daily; Petit Express does not save a step history or cumulative progress.

The face contains only Watch Face Format resources, without application code, a phone companion, an embedded SDK, network permission, advertising or analytics. Wear OS renders the count and appearance locally. Magic apps & games does not receive, upload, retain or share these values. Wear OS and the activity provider control data availability and manage their own permissions and privacy practices. Petit Express is not a medical device. Google Play handles purchases and downloads under its own privacy policy.

Tilo: steps on the watch

Tilo (com.observatoire.tilo) is a standalone Wear OS watch face. It uses the daily step count and goal supplied to its Watch Face Format complication only to show today’s progress and choose one of five illustrated moods for the same paper bird. The system’s daily count may reset; Tilo does not save a step history or cumulative growth, and the character never reverts to an egg or loses permanent identity.

The watch renders the count and goal locally. Tilo does not send or store them, request a health permission, or include advertising, analytics, a publisher account or a backend. The Watch Face Format package is resource-only, and the watch face works without a phone companion. The optional Tilo Android app displays this privacy summary and support contact. It only opens this policy in a browser or prepares a support email after the user taps; it has no Internet or health permission and does not read or transmit step data. Wear OS and the step-data provider have their own privacy practices and control data availability. Tilo is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Pulse Grid, Atlas Twin and Stride

These standalone watch faces (com.observatoire.pulsegrid, com.observatoire.atlastwin and com.observatoire.stride) have no phone companion, application code, analytics or advertising SDK, network permission or publisher account. Wear OS processes their appearance and settings locally. Pulse Grid displays system time, date and battery, with weather and steps when supplied by Wear OS or an installed provider. Stride displays time, date, steps, the heart rate value supplied by the selected health provider and weather when available; it does not independently measure heart rate. A progress indicator uses a goal supplied by the activity provider, and is omitted when a valid goal is unavailable. Atlas Twin displays system local time and the time and date for a city chosen in the editor. Its day/night marker is shown only when the watch weather source supplies that state; otherwise the sun and moon are decorative. Magic apps & games does not receive, upload, store or share these displayed values. Health and weather providers manage their own permissions and privacy practices. These watch faces do not diagnose or treat health conditions. The phone companion diagnostics below do not apply to them.

Information displayed on the watch

The watch face reads the time, date and battery information needed for its display. Depending on the design, it also displays step count, step goal, heart rate or weather information supplied by Wear OS or an installed provider such as Samsung Health, Samsung Weather or Google Weather.

These values are used to render the watch face. Magic apps & games does not receive, upload or maintain a history of these values. The watch face does not independently measure heart rate or request your location; weather providers manage their own location data and permissions.

Other phone installation companions

The installation companions for the other watch faces use Google's Wear services to identify connected watches and open the relevant Google Play listing on a chosen watch. Available watch names and connection identifiers are processed locally for this purpose. They are not sent to Magic apps & games or used to identify you across services. Opening the listing passes the store-opening request through Google's Wear services. Tilo's optional privacy app does not perform these functions.

There is no publisher account to create, no publisher backend or advertising SDK. Magic apps & games does not operate an analytics service in the companion. It does not upload watch health, weather or activity values. Purchases and downloads are handled by Google Play; the publisher does not receive your payment card details through these applications.

The Google Wear SDK used by those installation companions to open the store on your watch includes technical diagnostics for service calls, such as their result and duration. Processing of these diagnostics depends on Google Play services and its configuration. Magic apps & games does not receive these diagnostics. They are separate from the watch health, weather and activity values displayed by the watch face.

Other service providers

Google Play, Google's Wear services and the watch's weather or health providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their collection or processing is separate from the publisher's handling described here. You can manage their permissions and account settings in the relevant applications or device settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and the policies supplied by your watch's service providers.

Support email

If you email us, you voluntarily provide your email address, message and any attachments. We use them to respond to and resolve your request. Support messages are handled through Gmail and are not sold or used for advertising. Keep attachments limited to information needed to explain the issue.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. You can contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us. There is no app account or publisher-held watch-data history to delete.

This website

These collection, help and privacy pages do not add advertising, analytics, cookies or local-storage tracking. The language choice is carried in the page URL. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes; see GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Controls and updates

You can change source-app permissions in your device settings, remove the companion from your phone or uninstall the watch face. Contact us with privacy questions at abdo.elgomri@gmail.com. If these practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.

Paco : un compagnon animé, des données sur la montre
