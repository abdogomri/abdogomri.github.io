Privacy policy

7 November 2026 · Magic apps & games

Marine Crown uses the time, date, time format and battery level provided by your Wear OS watch to display the watch face. This information is displayed on the device.

The publisher does not collect, receive, store or share the displayed information. The watch face has no account, network permission, advertising or analytics SDK.

Google Play handles purchases and installation under its own privacy policy. You can remove the watch face through Wear OS. To ask about privacy or deletion, contact abdo.elgomri@gmail.com.
